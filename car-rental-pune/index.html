Car hire in Pune, the way you need it

Baste Travels is a Pune car rental service based in Hadapsar. Every rental comes with a verified driver, so you do not have to deal with Pune traffic, parking or highway navigation. You choose the car and the time; we handle the rest.

Local rental: 8 hours or 12 hours

Book a car and driver for a block of time inside Pune and PCMC: office visits across Kharadi and Hinjewadi, hospital appointments, shopping, family functions or a sightseeing day. The car stays with you and waits between stops. We quote the package up front on WhatsApp, including the kilometres covered and the rate for extra hours or kilometres.

Outstation trips
