Transport your teams can rely on

Baste Travels runs corporate transport from Hadapsar, minutes from Magarpatta City and SP Infocity and a short drive from the Kharadi IT corridor. We work with companies that need dependable shift transport without the overhead of managing vehicles and drivers themselves.

What we provide

  • Shift pickup and drop: fixed routes for morning, general and night shifts, with planned pickup points and times.
  • Executive and guest transfers: airport pickups, client visits and inter-office trips in sedans or premium sedans.
  • Outstation and offsites: SUVs and Tempo Travellers for team outings and offsites across Maharashtra.
  • Monthly dedicated vehicles: a car and driver assigned to your office. See monthly car rental.

Night-shift safety

Tell us your night-shift policy and we run trips to match it, for example routing so a woman employee is not the last drop, sharing live trip tracking with your transport desk, and having the driver wait until each employee is inside. Every driver is police-verified and follows our driver code of conduct and no-alcohol policy.

Billing and compliance

One monthly invoice with GST, trip logs on request, and a single point of contact for changes. Vehicles carry their commercial permit, insurance and fitness documents.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

What does employee transportation in Pune cost?

It depends on headcount, number of shifts, distances and vehicle type. Send your requirements through the form or WhatsApp and we prepare a monthly quote.

Do you provide GST invoices?

Yes. We bill monthly with GST and can share trip logs with each invoice.

How do you handle night shifts for women employees?

We follow your company's night-shift policy. Common measures we run are routing so a woman employee is not dropped last, sharing live tracking with your transport desk, and drivers waiting until the employee is safely inside.

Can you start with a small team?

Yes. We work with teams from a handful of employees to larger shift operations, and can add vehicles as you grow.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Pune and PCMC, including Kharadi, Magarpatta, Hadapsar, Viman Nagar, Yerawada, Hinjewadi, Baner and Wakad.
