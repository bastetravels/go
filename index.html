FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fare for Pune to Mumbai cab?

Baste Travels charges a flat rate of ₹12/km for Pune to Mumbai cab. The distance is approximately 150 km via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway. With a minimum billing of 300 km, the fare comes to approximately ₹3,600. Road tolls are paid separately at the booth.

Are the drivers police verified?

Yes. Every Baste Travels driver is independently police verified, holds a commercial LMV or PLV licence, and carries a medical fitness certificate. All 11 legal documents are present in the vehicle for every trip.

Is GPS tracking available during the trip?

Yes, every vehicle is equipped with live GPS tracking. You can share a real-time trip link with your family for complete peace of mind throughout the journey.

What is the minimum billing for outstation trips?

Minimum billing is 300 km at ₹12/km, making the minimum fare ₹3,600. This applies to all outstation trips from Pune including Mumbai, Nashik, Shirdi, Lonavala, and any city in Maharashtra.

Do you serve all cities in Maharashtra?

Yes. Baste Travels operates across all of Maharashtra — Tier-1 cities like Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur; Tier-2 cities like Nashik, Aurangabad, Kolhapur; pilgrimage sites like Shirdi, Trimbakeshwar, Pandharpur; hill stations like Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar; and every small town and village in the state.

Are road tolls included in the fare?

No. Road tolls (Expressway, inter-state, and highway tolls) are NOT included in our ₹12/km rate. Tolls are paid separately at the booth by the passenger. Everything else — driver, fuel, vehicle, insurance, AC, documents — is included.

What types of vehicles do you provide?

We primarily operate a brand-new Swift Dzire 2026 (sedan, 4 passengers, AC, spacious boot). For larger groups, we arrange Ertiga/Innova SUVs (6-7 seater) and Tempo Travellers (12-17 seater) on request. All vehicles are clean, maintained, and GPS-equipped.

How do I book a cab? Is there an app?

The quickest way is to call +91 75079 58364 or send a WhatsApp message. You can also fill the inquiry form on this page. We confirm your ride within minutes with driver and vehicle details. There is no app needed — just direct contact with the owner.

Do you offer corporate contracts and GST invoicing?

Yes. We offer monthly cab contracts for IT companies and corporates with employee pickup/drop, night-shift safe rides, and proper GST invoicing. Email us at info@bastetravels.com for corporate inquiries.
