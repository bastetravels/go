3. Vehicle Compliance — Maharashtra RTO & Yellow Plate Rules

🛑 All vehicles operated by Baste Travels are fully RTO-compliant:

3.1 Yellow Number Plate (Commercial Registration)

  • All vehicles carry yellow number plates with black lettering as mandated for commercial/contract carriage vehicles under Section 66 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988
  • Vehicles are registered as commercial transport vehicles under Maharashtra RTO with valid permits
  • Registration codes follow the Maharashtra format (MH-XX-XX-XXXX) with appropriate commercial series

3.2 HSRP (High Security Registration Plates)

  • All vehicles are fitted with HSRP plates as mandated by the Maharashtra Transport Department and MoRTH
  • HSRP plates include chromium-based hologram, laser-branded identification number, "IND" inscription, and non-removable snap-lock mechanism
  • Compliance verified as per the SOP issued by Maharashtra Transport Department (December 2024)

3.3 Permits & Certificates

  • Contract Carriage Permit: Valid permit under Section 74 of Motor Vehicles Act for all vehicles
  • Fitness Certificate: Valid and renewed as per Maharashtra RTO schedule
  • Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate: Valid for all vehicles
  • Comprehensive Insurance: Third-party and comprehensive motor insurance for every vehicle
  • Passenger Insurance: Coverage for all passengers during the ride
  • Road Tax: Paid in full as per Maharashtra Motor Vehicle Taxation Act
  • Vehicle Age: No vehicle older than 10 years is used in our fleet

3.4 Vehicle Standards

  • All vehicles meet BS-VI emission norms
  • Air-conditioning, first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, and dashcam in every vehicle
  • Daily hygiene check, weekly deep cleaning, monthly mechanical inspection
  • Speedometers and GPS devices calibrated and functional at all times

4. Driver Compliance

  • All drivers hold a valid commercial driving licence (yellow badge / transport licence) issued by an Indian RTO
  • Minimum 3 years of professional driving experience required
  • Police verification clearance from Maharashtra Police / local police station
  • Medical fitness certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner
  • Drug & alcohol test clearance
  • Mandatory completion of Baste safety & sensitivity training program
  • Aadhaar-verified identity linked to our system

5. Service Terms

5.1 Booking & Scheduling

  • Services can be booked via WhatsApp (+91 7507958364), phone call, email (bastetravels@gmail.com), or through the Baste Travels App (when available)
  • Corporate contracts are formalized through a written agreement specifying routes, schedules, vehicle type, pricing, and terms
  • Individual bookings are confirmed upon acknowledgment via WhatsApp or phone

5.2 Ride Policies

  • Passengers must be present at the designated pickup point at the scheduled time
  • A waiting time of 10 minutes is provided free of charge; beyond that, waiting charges apply as per the contract
  • Route changes during a ride must be communicated to the driver and may incur additional charges
  • Maximum passenger capacity as per vehicle type must be respected — no overloading
  • Seatbelts must be worn by all passengers at all times

5.3 Prohibited Activities During Rides

  • Consumption of alcohol, drugs, or any intoxicating substance
  • Smoking, vaping, or use of tobacco products inside the vehicle
  • Carrying illegal items, weapons, explosives, or hazardous materials
  • Abusive, threatening, or inappropriate behavior towards the driver
  • Damaging, defacing, or soiling the vehicle interior
  • Travelling with pets without prior approval

6. Pricing & Payment

  • All pricing is agreed upon in advance — either through the corporate contract or per-booking confirmation
  • No surge pricing — prices are fixed as per agreed rates
  • Corporate clients receive monthly invoices with detailed trip reports and GST-compliant billing
  • Individual bookings can be paid via UPI, bank transfer, or cash (as agreed)
  • GST is charged as applicable under the GST Act, 2017 for transportation services
  • All invoices carry valid GSTIN of Baste Travels

7. Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • Cancellations made 2+ hours before the scheduled ride: No charge
  • Cancellations made less than 2 hours before: 25% of ride fare
  • Cancellations after driver dispatch / no-show: 50% of ride fare
  • Corporate contract cancellations are governed by the specific contract terms
  • Rescheduling is free if communicated at least 1 hour before the ride
