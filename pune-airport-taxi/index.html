Airport transfers to and from Lohegaon

Pune International Airport (PNQ) is in Lohegaon, north-east of the city. Baste Travels runs airport pickups and drops around the clock from our base in Hadapsar, with most trips starting in east Pune: Hadapsar, Magarpatta, Kharadi, Viman Nagar and Koregaon Park.

Arriving in Pune

Send us your flight number when you book. We track the flight, so if it lands late your driver adjusts and you are not charged for the delay. Your driver's name and number reach you on WhatsApp before you land, and the driver meets you outside arrivals.

Flying out of Pune

For domestic flights, airlines generally ask you to be at the terminal two hours before departure; for international flights, three hours. We plan the pickup time with you based on your area and the time of day, with a buffer for traffic on Nagar Road and the airport approach.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

How early should I leave for Pune Airport?

Airlines generally ask you to reach the terminal two hours before a domestic flight and three hours before an international one. From Hadapsar or Magarpatta, plan 30 to 45 minutes of driving; from Hinjewadi or Baner, plan more at peak hours. We suggest the pickup time when you book.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight number, so your driver arrives for the actual landing time. You are not charged for a delay in the flight.

Where does the driver meet me?

Outside the arrivals exit. Your driver calls you once you are through baggage claim.

How much is a taxi from Hadapsar to Pune Airport?

Airport fares depend on the exact pickup address, time and car. WhatsApp us your address and flight time and we send the full fare in minutes.

Do you have bigger cars for families?

Yes. Ertiga or Innova Crysta for 6 to 7 people and a Tempo Traveller for 12 to 17. See our fleet page.
