Pune to Mumbai Airport, without the stress

Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) is roughly 150–170 km from Pune depending on where you start, almost all of it on the Mumbai–Pune Expressway. International flights use Terminal 2 (T2); domestic flights use T1 or T2 depending on the airline. Tell us your airline and flight number and we drop you at the right terminal.

Fare: ₹12/km with a 300 km minimum billing, so a one-way Pune to Mumbai Airport trip is ₹3,600. Expressway and city tolls are paid separately at the booth. AC, driver and fuel are included.

When to leave Pune

The drive usually takes 3 to 4 hours, longer at Mumbai peak hours or in heavy monsoon rain. Add the airline's reporting time on top: about two hours for domestic and three hours for international flights. As a rule of thumb, leave Pune 6 to 7 hours before an international departure and 5 to 6 hours before a domestic one. We confirm a pickup time with you when you book.
