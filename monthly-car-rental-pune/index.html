A car on call, every day of the month

Monthly rental gives you the convenience of your own car and driver without the purchase, insurance, maintenance or driver management. Baste Travels assigns a car and a regular driver for your contract period, based out of our Hadapsar office.

Who it suits

  • Executives and expats on a Pune assignment who need daily office travel and weekend trips.
  • Families with elderly parents, school runs or regular hospital visits.
  • NRIs visiting Pune for a few weeks or months.
  • Businesses that need a dedicated vehicle for a manager, a site or a project. For shift transport see employee transportation.

How the monthly package works

We build the package around how you will use the car: the number of days per month, daily duty hours and expected kilometres. The quote states clearly what is included (car, driver, fuel within the package, maintenance) and the rate for extra hours or kilometres, so your monthly bill is predictable. Tolls and parking during your trips are charged as actuals.

Need it for less than a month? Use our 8 hr or 12 hr local rental by the day instead.

Choosing the car

Most monthly clients choose a sedan such as the Swift Dzire. Families and executives often prefer an SUV (Ertiga or Innova Crysta) or a premium sedan (Honda City or Ciaz). Compare them on the fleet page.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

What is the minimum period for monthly rental?

One month. Shorter needs are better served by our daily 8 hr or 12 hr local packages.

Is the driver the same every day?

We assign a regular driver for your contract. On the driver's weekly off or leave we send a trained, verified replacement.

Is fuel included?

The monthly package is quoted with fuel for the agreed kilometres. Kilometres beyond the package are billed at the rate stated in your quote.

Can a company take a monthly car?

Yes. We issue a monthly GST invoice in the company name.

Can I use the car outside Pune?

Yes, with notice. Outstation days are billed as agreed in your package, plus tolls.
