How outstation pricing works

Baste Travels charges a flat ₹12 per km for outstation trips, with a minimum billing of 300 km. Shorter trips are billed as 300 km (₹3,600); longer trips are billed on actual distance. Tolls are paid separately at the booth. There is no surge at night, on weekends or during festivals.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

What is the outstation cab rate from Pune?

A flat ₹12 per km with a 300 km minimum billing (₹3,600). Tolls are extra.

Is there a one-way cab from Pune?

Yes. One-way drops are billed on the one-way distance, subject to the 300 km minimum.

Are tolls and parking included?

No. Tolls and parking are paid separately as actuals. Fuel, driver and AC are included.

Do you charge extra at night or on festivals?

No. The rate is the same 24×7, including festivals and long weekends.

Which car do I get at ₹12/km?

Our standard sedan, usually a Swift Dzire, for up to 4 passengers. Larger vehicles are quoted on request.
